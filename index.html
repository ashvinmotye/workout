    How to interpret load, RPE, zones and comparisons
    Session loadDuration in minutes × RPE. It estimates total internal effort; it is not calories, and a larger score is not automatically better.
    RPEYour whole-session effort from 1–10. Compare it alongside completed work: the same work at a lower RPE can suggest improving tolerance.
    Heart-rate zonesZ1–Z2 are easier aerobic work, Z3 is sustained tempo, and Z4–Z5 are hard efforts. The right distribution depends on the session goal.
    ComparisonsThe arrow shows previous → current. “pp” means percentage points. Work, RPE, load and zone share are interpreted together rather than judged independently.
