# Forge icon

The Forge brand mark is an open ring of dark forged steel with a deliberate
gap at the top-right. Blue and cyan AuraOS energy travels from the left through
the lower arc, expressing strength, motion, adaptation, consistency and
continuous progress.

## Reusable generation prompt

Create a premium square app icon for a workout and progression app named
**Forge**.

The central emblem is a thick **open circular ring made from dark forged brushed
steel**. The ring has a deliberate gap at the **top-right around the 1–2 o’clock
position**.

A dynamic stream of luminous energy travels around the ring, strongest from the
**left side through the lower-left and bottom arc**, as though energy is actively
forging or transforming the metal.

Use the **AuraOS Arctic Depth** color language instead of traditional orange
fire. The energy should glow in deep blue, vivid electric blue, cyan and subtle
teal, inspired by `#065b98`, `#1b7fdc`, and `#087d95`. The brightest areas may
approach cool white.

Add subtle blue sparks, particles and reflected light along the forged metal
surface.

The background should be a deep blue-black rounded square with restrained
texture and enough contrast for the ring to remain immediately recognizable.

The metal should feel heavy, tactile and slightly weathered, with realistic
forged/brushed texture rather than polished chrome.

The overall image should communicate **strength, motion, adaptation, consistency
and continuous progress**.

Premium modern AuraOS aesthetic, slightly futuristic and cinematic, highly
legible as an iOS/PWA app icon, centered composition, no text, no letters, no
dumbbells, no flames, no hammer, no generic fitness imagery, no orange or red
glow.

## Included icon matrix

- `icons/forge-icon-master.png` — 1254 × 1254 source master
- `icons/apple-touch-icon.png` — 180 × 180 Apple touch icon
- `icons/icon-192.png` and `icons/icon-512.png` — standard PWA icons
- `icons/icon-maskable-192.png` and `icons/icon-maskable-512.png` — maskable PWA
  icons with additional safe-area padding
- `icons/favicon-16.png`, `icons/favicon-32.png`, and `icons/favicon-48.png`
- `icons/favicon.ico` — combined 16, 32 and 48 px favicon

All production icons are opaque sRGB images. Standard icons use the complete
master composition. Maskable icons place a reduced master on the same deep
blue-black background to protect the ring from operating-system cropping.
