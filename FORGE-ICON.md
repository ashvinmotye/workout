# Forge icon

The Version 29 Forge mark uses the supplied Fabric Design System dumbbell as a
direct strength symbol. It is centered diagonally and recolored with the AuraOS
Arctic Depth palette: `#065b98`, `#1b7fdc`, `#087d95`, cyan and cool-white
highlights. A blue/cyan energy sweep crosses the lower arc to preserve Forge's
language of motion, adaptation and continuous progress.

The background is a deep blue-black forged panel with restrained brushed
texture. Its lighter metallic rounded-square frame uses iOS-compatible corner
geometry so the Home Screen squircle does not cut through the frame.

## Editable sources

- `icons/icon-source.svg` — complete 1024 × 1024 AuraOS icon composition
- `icons/dumbbell-mark.svg` — original supplied Fabric Design System mark,

## Included icon matrix

- `icons/forge-icon-master.png` — 1024 × 1024 rendered master
- `icons/apple-touch-icon-v29.png` — versioned 180 × 180 Apple Touch icon
- `icons/apple-touch-icon.png` — matching compatibility copy
- `icons/icon-192.png` and `icons/icon-512.png` — standard PWA icons
- `icons/icon-maskable-192.png` and `icons/icon-maskable-512.png` — maskable PWA
  icons with additional safe-area padding
- `icons/favicon-16.png`, `icons/favicon-32.png`, and `icons/favicon-48.png`
- `icons/favicon.ico` — combined 16, 32 and 48 px favicon

All production icons are opaque sRGB images. Standard icons use the complete
master composition. Maskable icons place a reduced master on the same deep
blue-black background to protect the dumbbell and frame from operating-system
cropping.
